ResearchQueen's University

Undergraduate Research Assistant

with Dr. Erin Meger · April 2026 to present

I work on pursuit-evasion games on connected graphs, specifically the Cops, Robbers, and Barricades (CRB) model, where players can block vertices as well as move. I built a browser-based graph engine to visualize and solve these games, and wrote a closed-form equation for the cop-barricade number on complete staged bipartite graphs, a novel result.
